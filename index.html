Welcome

Welcome to Guardian 4 Heroes, where healing and growth meet the power of the horse. Our nonprofit is dedicated to equine-facilitated psychotherapy and assisted training, offering a compassionate, supportive environment for veterans and first responders on their journey to wellness.

Empowering Journeys Begin

Discover the transformative power of EFPT at Guardian 4 Heroes. Our programs harness the power of horses to support mental health and physical well-being. Join us on a journey of healing and hope.

When the noise of war fades, the silence can be loud. But here, with every hoofbeat and shared breath, healing begins.
