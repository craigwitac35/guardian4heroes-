Healing through Connection, Balance, and Purpose

At Guardian 4 Heroes, we believe healing begins with connection: to self, to others, and to the world around us. Our Equine Facilitated Psychotherapy, Sound Therapy, and wellness programs are designed to support anyone seeking emotional, mental, or physical well-being. This includes those navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, or new to sobriety.

Our compassionate facilitators guide you through safe, hands-on experiences that allow healing to unfold naturally. Our sliding-scale pricing ensures accessibility for all, because healing should never depend on your current financial situation.

Our Core Therapy Programs

We offer services for Veterans, First Responders, Athletes, and our entire community. We are now accepting new clients for all programs. Our core sessions are offered on a sliding scale to ensure access to care.

Equine-Facilitated Psychotherapy & Learning (EFPT)

(Individuals, or couples/family sessions) - Sara has 3 certifications

Work hands-on with our horses in a safe, therapeutic setting. EFPT is designed for those navigating mental health challenges or who are new to sobriety and looking to reconnect. No prior horse experience is needed.

In this program, you will:

  • Build confidence, trust, and self-awareness
  • Practice healthy boundaries and communication skills
  • Develop emotional regulation and find a sense of peace

Session Length: 45 minutes
Rates: $80.00 per session (Sliding scale available)

Sound Therapy with BioTune

(Individual or group sessions) - Sara is certified

Experience the transformative power of sound. BioTune technology blends vibration, frequency, and rhythm to help you find calm, reconnection, and healing.

These sessions are personalized to support your unique needs:

  • Release tension and balance your body's energy
  • Promote deep relaxation and calm the nervous system
  • Support healing from PTSD, depression, and anxiety

Session Length: 40-60 minutes
Rates: $40-$60 per session (Sliding scale available)

Why Horses?

Horses are incredibly responsive to human emotion. Their ability to mirror our non-verbal cues creates opportunities for self-discovery and therapeutic growth. They offer honest, immediate feedback and provide a unique path to healing and self-understanding.
